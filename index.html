Health — Hydration

Top 7 Water Brands Ranked by Purity

💧
1

Penta Ultra-Purified

A 13-step solar-powered purification process removes virtually every contaminant that exists. TDS under 10 ppm. No detectable heavy metals in any third-party lab test we found. If pure means pure then Penta wins on science alone. The premium price reflects a premium process that no other mainstream brand matches.

Shop This Pick
2

Essentia Ionized Alkaline

Ionized at 9.5 pH with TDS under 10 ppm and no detectable heavy metals in independent testing. The alkaline angle has genuine hydration science behind it — higher pH water may help neutralize acid load in the body after exercise. Widely available, more affordable than Penta, and one of the cleanest options at any grocery store.

Shop This Pick
3

Icelandic Glacial

Filtered through volcanic lava rock for decades before being bottled. Naturally alkaline at pH 8.4. Non-detectable arsenic and lead in independent testing. The source — an ancient spring in Iceland — is one of the most pristine water sources on earth. What the ground does naturally here no industrial process fully replicates.

Shop This Pick
4

Voss Still Water

The lowest TDS of any mainstream spring water at just 22 mg/L. That mineral content is so low the water tastes exceptionally clean and neutral — which is exactly why water connoisseurs and chefs prefer it. The iconic cylindrical bottle is not just aesthetic — it is glass or BPA-free plastic protecting the purity inside.

Shop This Pick
5

Crystal Geyser Alpine Spring

A real alpine spring source bottled at the source — not processed municipal water dressed up in premium packaging. The most affordable genuine spring water on the market and the personal go-to pick from the Tops team. Bay Area local. Clean ingredient list. Does exactly what spring water should do at a price that does not require a second thought.

Shop This Pick
6

Evian Natural Spring

Filtered through the French Alps for over 15 years before reaching the bottle. Zero measurable arsenic in independent testing — important context given what happened when Fiji was tested in 2006. Evian has consistently clean third-party lab results across decades of testing. The European standard that has held up under scrutiny.

Shop This Pick
7

FIJI Natural Artesian Water

Volcanic rock filtered artesian water with a unique silica profile that genuinely tastes different from anything else. FIJI earns its place here on taste and source quality — but transparency requires the full story. In 2006 FIJI ran an ad mocking Cleveland's tap water. Cleveland tested FIJI in response and found 6.31 micrograms of arsenic per liter. Aquafina, Dasani, and Evian all had zero measurable arsenic in the same test. The good news — more recent independent testing shows FIJI's arsenic levels now at approximately 1 microgram per bottle, well within FDA limits of 10 micrograms per liter. The company has improved. The history is still real. Do your own research and make an informed choice.

🏆 Tops Honest Note — FIJI improved significantly but their 2006 arsenic incident is documented fact. We include them because the product is good and the levels are now safe. We note the history because you deserve to know it.

Bay Area — Music

Top 5 Bay Area Artists of All Time

🌉
1

Tupac Shakur

Oakland born. Attended Fremont High School in Oakland before moving to Baltimore and eventually Marin City. The most influential rapper in history started right here. The combination of political consciousness, raw emotion, and lyrical precision that defined Tupac's work has never been replicated. Everything Bay Area hip hop built was built on his foundation.

Shop Vinyl
2

E-40

Vallejo legend. 35 years deep in the game and still relevant. Created his own slang — many Bay Area words and phrases now used nationally started with E-40. Built his own label. Carved his own lane before independent artists understood what independence meant. Nobody in hip hop has maintained a career at this level from a regional base for this long.

Shop Vinyl
3

Too Short

Oakland original who laid the groundwork for West Coast rap before anyone was paying attention. Three decades of consistency in an industry where careers last three years. Too Short was independent before independent was a strategy. He owned his masters before artists knew to fight for them. The Bay Area rap blueprint starts here.

Shop Album
4

Mac Dre

The Vallejo king who created the hyphy movement — a Bay Area cultural phenomenon that influenced music, fashion, dance, and identity across Northern California. Gone too soon in 2004 but the culture he built never stopped growing. The Bay has never forgotten and the hyphy movement he started is still alive in the music, the slang, and the spirit of the region.

5

MC Hammer

Oakland born and the first Bay Area artist to go truly global. Please Hammer Don't Hurt Em sold over 10 million copies and was the best-selling rap album of 1990. Hammer took the Bay Area sound to number one on the charts worldwide at a time when nobody outside the region was paying attention. He opened the door that everyone who followed walked through.

Sneakers — All Time

Top 5 Sneakers of All Time

👟
1

Air Jordan 1

Started a cultural movement in 1985 that still runs the world 40 years later. Michael Jordan wore them. The NBA banned them. Nike paid the fines and kept selling them. That story alone made them legendary before a single consumer bought a pair. The colorway range across four decades means they are simultaneously the most historic and the most current sneaker in existence. No other shoe in history has maintained this level of cultural relevance for this long.

Shop This Pick
2

New Balance 574

The most slept on classic in sneaker history — and the people who know have always known. While everyone chased Jordans and Nikes the 574 quietly built a fanbase of people with genuine taste. The silhouette ages better with every passing decade. The comfort is unmatched for an everyday sneaker. It went from dad shoe to the most desired silhouette in fashion in a single cultural shift because the foundation was always right.

Shop This Pick
3

Adidas Samba OG

From the indoor soccer pitch in 1949 to every major city on earth in 2026. The Samba traveled from athletics to fashion to streetwear and never lost its identity in any of those transitions. The gum sole, the T-toe cap, the low profile — everything about this shoe is exactly right and has been for 75 years. The people's shoe of this generation and it earned that title without a celebrity campaign.

Shop This Pick
4

Nike Dunk Low

Defined the streetwear era more completely than almost any other product in fashion history. Originally released as a basketball shoe in 1985, shelved for decades, revived in the early 2000s through skateboarding, and exploded into mainstream culture through limited colorway drops that created lines around the block. Still impossible to find in the best colorways because demand will always exceed supply for the ones that matter.

Shop This Pick
5

Nike Air Force 1

40 years old in 2022. Still the most consistent sneaker ever made. The Air Force 1 was the first basketball shoe to use Nike Air cushioning in 1982 and never stopped selling since. It has never gone out of style because it has never been in style in a trend sense — it exists outside of trends entirely. The triple white colorway alone has sold tens of millions of units across four decades. The most reliable sneaker in existence.

Travel — Sports

Top 5 NFL Stadiums to Experience Live

🏈
1

Lambeau Field — Green Bay

The most historic stadium in American sports. Walking into Lambeau is like walking into football history — the frozen tundra, the Titletown atmosphere, the Packers faithful who have waited years for their seats. There is no stadium in American sports that carries more weight per square foot than this one. Dec 13 vs the Bills.

✈️ Fly There
2

Allegiant Stadium — Las Vegas

The most technologically advanced stadium ever built. Raiders in the desert with the Vegas Strip as the backdrop. The retractable roof and natural grass field that rolls in from outside are engineering achievements. A game here plus a Vegas weekend is the best sports travel package in the NFL. Thursday Night Football this season.

✈️ Fly There
3

Arrowhead Stadium — Kansas City

Consistently the loudest stadium in NFL history by independent measurement. Chiefs Kingdom is a real phenomenon — the crowd energy affects opposing offenses in ways that show up in statistics. Dec 27 vs the 49ers is a late season potential playoff preview. Southwest flies there from the Bay for $248 round trip.

✈️ Fly There
4

Lincoln Financial Field — Philadelphia

The most passionate fan base in football. Philadelphia fans are not casual — they are invested at a level that changes the atmosphere of every game. Now with LeBron James in the city adding celebrity to the energy around the 76ers the entire Philadelphia sports culture is elevated. Oct 4 vs the LA Rams. $349 round trip nonstop.

✈️ Fly There
5

Levi's Stadium — Santa Clara 🌉

Bay Area football. The World Cup was just played here. The most scenic venue in the NFL with views of the South Bay hills. If you are already in the Bay take BART to Warm Springs then VTA light rail direct to the stadium. No parking. No stress. Leave at the two-minute warning or 101 will humble you every single time.
